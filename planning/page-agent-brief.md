# Brief for page-building agents

You are adding pages to the Indiana County Civic Guide (St. Joseph County edition): a nonpartisan, source-cited, multi-page civic-education site generated by Python from data. Several agents build different sections **in parallel**, so follow the parallel-safe rules exactly.

## Read first (`C:\Users\nickb\local-gov-guide\`)
1. `tools\site\README.md`, especially "Parallel-safe conventions" at the end. **Never edit `build.py`, `static/style.css`, `static/app.js`, or other agents' `pages/*.py`.** Register pages with `PAGES = {...}` in your own module. Put styles in `static/css/<area>.css` and scripts in `static/js/<area>.js`.
2. Study `tools\site\pages\county.py` as the reference implementation (components, charts, citations, gaps, badges, variants, tables with Copy CSV).
3. `planning\build-plan.md`, `planning\artifact-contract.md` (hosting rules), `planning\ux-review.md` §3 (IA, paths), §5.2–5.3 (generic vs county content, variant slots) and §6 (neutrality checklist, badges).
4. `data\SCHEMA.md`, then the data your pages need in `data\indiana\` and `data\editions\st-joseph\`. Also `planning\fact-check-report.md`; never show anything it marked REMOVE or anything uncorrected.

## Rules
- **Every factual sentence is backed by a data row or source** and cited with the citation component. Generic Indiana explanations come from `data\indiana\*.csv` plain-language fields. No invented facts, no facts "from memory". If something is missing, show it in the page's Gaps list instead of writing around it.
- **Status badges** on data (official / unofficial / computed / from scan / secondary / single source). Low-confidence values never appear in headings, chart titles or landing summaries; show them only in tables, flagged.
- **Neutral language.** No "flip", "sweep", "landslide", "seized", "won back", "wave", "safe", "target", "vulnerable", "battleground", "toss-up", no praise or criticism adjectives, and no causal claims about why people voted as they did. Order lists by office/district/date or alphabetically, never by party. Treat parties symmetrically. Party colors only on party data, always with a text label.
- **Scope badges:** INDIANA-WIDE vs ST. JOSEPH COUNTY vs ST. JOSEPH EXCEPTION (via edition.json variants; show unverified variants as unverified).
- **Charts** use the toolkit in `civic/` (inline SVG, tokens, tooltips, a table under each chart). Full available range, one axis, series breaks drawn on the chart. Non-party charts never use party colors.
- **2026 general results are UNOFFICIAL**; terms starting 2027-01-01 show as "Takes office Jan 1, 2027 (results unofficial)".
- **Plain, direct copy** a high-schooler can follow. Short sentences. Name things by what residents recognize.
- Every page ends with Sources and a Gaps list. Pages must work at 400 px wide, in light and dark.

## Build and check
`python -P -E tools\site\build.py --edition st-joseph --check` must PASS, with your pages not listed under FAILED. If the build fails because of ANOTHER agent's module, don't touch it: wait a minute and rebuild (the build skips broken modules). The preview server (pm2 `civic-guide-preview`) serves `site\st-joseph\` at http://localhost:8787. **Do not use the browser for screenshots**: other agents share it, and the main session does the visual review. You may fetch your pages over HTTP (curl) to sanity-check the HTML.
