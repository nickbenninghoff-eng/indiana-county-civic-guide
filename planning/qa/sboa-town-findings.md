## 76373A - Town of Osceola

- Report type as printed: "COMPLIANCE ENGAGEMENT REPORT". Period: January 1, 2020 to December 31, 2024. Filed: 05/15/2025.
- Auditor: Indiana State Board of Accounts. Compliance report signed Beth Kelley, CPA, CFE, Deputy State Examiner, April 16, 2025 (p. 4); State Examiner on cover: Paul D. Joyce, CPA.
- Opinion: none. Report says "we do not provide any opinions on compliance or financial activity" (p. 3). The Statements of Receipts, Disbursements, and Schedule of Cash and Investment Balances "have not been included in this report due to the issues detailed in the comments below" (p. 3).
- Comments (pp. 5-12). No classification (material weakness / significant deficiency) is printed anywhere; the report uses "Comments" and "internal control deficiencies resulted in noncompliance over" five items (p. 5):
  1. "INTERNAL CONTROLS - COMPLIANCE" (p. 5): umbrella comment listing the five items below.
  2. "BANK ACCOUNT RECONCILIATIONS" (pp. 5-7): The Town prepared reconciliations, but each December reconciliation (2020-2023) included unsupported adjustments (items that had already cleared or were also in the outstanding list). With them removed, the ledger exceeded the adjusted bank balance by $20 at 12/31/2020, and the adjusted bank balance exceeded the ledger by $500 (2021), $9,191 (2022) and $21,166 (2023); four of five additional reconciliations tested contained previously cleared items; 12/31/2024 reconciled as of January 20, 2025 "with supported adjustments". Amounts: 2020 deposits in transit $20; 2021 five deposits $90 and one check $500; 2022 four deposits $107 and 26 checks $66,313; 2023 four deposits $102, eight checks $21,267, one adjustment $350, eight adjustments $56,668.
  3. "ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT" (pp. 7-8): AFRs for 2020-2024 "contained errors and did not accurately reflect the financial activity of the Town"; because of the above, the Statements were not included. Specific errors: 2024 accounts payable/receivable reported as total receipts and disbursements; capital assets in AFR overstated $14,555 (2020, 2021, 2022), understated $209,301 (2023), overstated $72,932 (2024); 2021 grant schedule included $77,577 of receipts of which $76,070 was received and reported in the prior year, overstating the AFR by $76,070.
  4. "ACCOUNTING OF THE IFA ADMINISTERED CRF MONEY" (pp. 8-10): The Town did not account for the Coronavirus Relief Fund per State Examiner Directive 2020-3. 2020 year-end CARES fund balance $76,070; IFA reimbursements of $75,593 (public health and safety payroll) received Nov-Dec 2020 and $477 (non-payroll) Dec 2020 were receipted into the CARES fund without the reversing/claim steps; $1,507 more received January 2021; all moved to the General fund in October 2021 leaving a zero balance.
  5. "MOTOR VEHICLE HIGHWAY (MVH) - RESTRICTED FUND" (p. 10): The Town created the MVH Restricted fund but did not allocate at least 50 percent of MVH distributions to it at receipt; $5,043 was receipted into the MVH fund that should have gone to MVH Restricted.
  6. "FUND SOURCES AND USES" (pp. 10-12): In a test of 35 disbursements: four purchases included sales tax; one purchase paid utilities from the Law Enforcement Continuing Education fund ("unallowable per Indiana Code"); two purchases were gift cards on the Town credit card, "not a permissible use" under Town Resolution 08-2014; three purchases were meals on the Town credit card "with no identified Town business or travel purpose". No dollar amounts stated.
- Prior-report references: none printed (no "similar comment appeared" sentence found; searched "similar", "prior", "same comment").
- Official response / corrective action plan: none included. The text says only that "Any Official Response to the comments incorporated within this report was not verified for accuracy" (p. 3).
- Exit conference (p. 4): contents communicated April 30, 2025 to: Clerk-Treasurer; President of the Town Council; Town Council member; Town Council member; Deputy Clerk. Also communicated May 5, 2025 to the former Clerk-Treasurer.
- Amounts: see items 2, 3, 4, 5 above. Charges against individuals: none stated.

## 78484A - Town of Roseland

- Report type as printed: "COMPLIANCE ENGAGEMENT REPORT". Period: January 1, 2020 to December 31, 2023. Filed: 12/02/2024.
- Auditor: Indiana State Board of Accounts. Signed Beth Kelley, CPA, CFE, Deputy State Examiner, November 5, 2024 (p. 4).
- Opinion: none ("we do not provide any opinions on compliance or financial activity", p. 3). Statements of Receipts, Disbursements, and Cash and Investment Balances (pp. 14-15) "are informational only" with no assurance.
- Comments (pp. 5-11). No material weakness/significant deficiency classification printed.
  1. "ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT" (pp. 5-6). Prior-report reference: "A similar comment also appeared in prior Reports B49432 and B56373, entitled INTERNAL CONTROLS." Condition: AFR entered in Gateway "without a documented oversight, review, or approval process"; the 2023 AFR was filed 41 days after its due date. Errors: Playground Donations Fund (created 2023) not reported in the 2023 AFR, understating receipts $7,652, disbursements $1,622, ending cash $6,030; 2023 MVH disbursements understated $1,246 and ending cash overstated $1,246; grant info: 2021 insurance amount overstated by $100,000 for each of two grants (CRF and SLFRF); 2022 SLFRF insurance overstated by $1,000,000 and amount passed to subrecipients overstated by $72,339; capital asset amounts not supported by a detailed record. "Adjustments ... were proposed, accepted by the Town, and made".
  2. "INTERNAL CONTROLS" (pp. 6-7): umbrella listing Supporting Documentation, Bank Account Reconciliations, Fund Sources and Uses - CEDIT Transfers, Monthly and Annual Uploads, Capital Assets, Subsequent Events.
  3. "SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION" (p. 7): of 20 disbursement claims tested, support could not be provided for 3.
  4. "BANK ACCOUNT RECONCILIATIONS" (p. 7): of ten monthly reconciliations tested, three were not timely (completed six to nine months after month end); the 12/31/2023 reconciliation listed an unknown reconciling item of $601 with no support, leaving the bank balance above the fund ledger balance.
  5. "FUND SOURCES AND USES - CEDIT TRANSFERS" (pp. 8-9): The Town annually transferred CEDIT funds to the General Fund and the MVH fund, "not in compliance with laws and regulations"; "$366,000 was transferred out of the CEDIT fund over the engagement period."
  6. "MONTHLY AND ANNUAL UPLOADS" (p. 9): Town did not upload the December 2023 bank statement, reconciliation and fund ledger; any required annual files for 2023; or any required monthly files for 2024.
  7. "CAPITAL ASSETS" (pp. 9-10): The Town could not provide a detailed capital asset record to support AFR amounts, and the examiners could not determine whether a complete annual physical inventory per Town policy was taken.
  8. "SUBSEQUENT EVENTS" (pp. 10-11). Prior-report reference: "A similar comment also appeared in prior Report B56373, entitled ERRORS ON CLAIMS." Condition: as of October 2024 the Town kept no record of any 2024 Town Council meeting minutes; examiners could not tell whether claim dockets were presented for Council approval, "and, therefore, checks were being drawn against the funds without proper approval."
- Official response / corrective action plan: none included (only the standard "not verified for accuracy" sentence, p. 3).
- Exit conference (p. 4): communicated November 15, 2024 to: former Clerk-Treasurer; Clerk-Treasurer; President of the Town Council; Town Council member.
- Amounts: $7,652 / $1,622 / $6,030; $1,246; $100,000; $1,000,000; $72,339; $601; $366,000 (CEDIT transfers). No amounts charged personally. UNSURE: pages 11-12 of the printed sequence contain only the end of the Subsequent Events criteria and a blank/footer page; no further comments follow.
