Winners: Maritza Robles and John Anella.** Confidence: medium-high (statute high; winners medium: Ballotpedia via search snippet only, corroborated by ABC57 2020 "newcomer").

Evidence:
1. Statute IC 20-23-15-6 (2016 Indiana Code, Justia PDF https://statecodesfiles.justia.com/indiana/2016/title-20/article-23/chapter-15/chapter-15.pdf): "(a) The governing body of the school corporation consists of seven (7) members ... (b) Five (5) of the members shall be elected from the school districts ... (c) Two (2) of the members shall be elected at large."
