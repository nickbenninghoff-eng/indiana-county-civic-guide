## 1. Mishawaka services (filled)

Source: city pages under https://mishawaka.in.gov (departments list read via WebFetch; subpages via WebFetch summary).
- Streets, snowplowing, trash/recycling, leaves, traffic signs: **Central Services Division**, 700 Union St, (574) 258-1660, Mon-Fri 8:30-4:45. Page lists "snowplowing" and "curbside leaf collection".
- Trash/recycling problems: report to Republic Services via link on the city's Report a Problem page ("Trash and recycling problems can now be reported directly to Republic Services").
- Water, sewer, electric: **Mishawaka Utilities** (Electric, Water, Wastewater Treatment divisions), Business Office (574) 258-1630. Electrical outages (574) 258-1649. Complaints can go to the Utility Board.
- Streetlights: no maintaining department named. "street lights" is a category on the online Report a Problem form (also snow removal, potholes, drainage, illegal dumping, traffic signs/signals). Low confidence on who maintains them.
- Building permits: **Mishawaka Building Department**, 100 Lincolnway West, (574) 258-1607, 7:30-4:45; paper forms, no online portal mentioned.
- Parks: **Parks and Recreation**, 904 N. Main St (Battell Center), (574) 258-1664; city Board of Parks and Recreation.
- Planning/zoning: **Planning and Community Development**, (574) 258-1625; staffs Plan Commission and BZA; zoning ordinance on Municode.
- Non-emergency police (574) 258-1678. Report a Problem URL: https://mishawaka.in.gov/our-community/residents/residential-services/report-a-problem/ ; "If you'd like, you can remain anonymous."
- Mishawaka has no 311/app mentioned.

## 2. Planning and zoning (APC vs cities)

County page (exact, Chrome) https://www.sjcindiana.gov/2142/About-Us- : "The Area Plan Commission serves the following jurisdictions: St. Joseph County (unincorporated 5 digit addresses) Town of Lakeville Town of New Carlisle Town of North Liberty Town of Osceola Town of Roseland". So the APC does **not** cover South Bend, Mishawaka, Walkerton or Indian Village. A search-engine summary claimed it covers South Bend; the county's own page contradicts that. The 2019 APC annual report excerpt listed the South Bend mayor's appointee, but 2021 and 2026 do not: South Bend left.
- Mishawaka: own Plan Commission (meets 2nd Tuesday 7 pm if business) and BZA (2nd Tuesday 6 pm), staffed by city Planning.
- South Bend: own Plan Commission and BZA (southbendin.gov/board: BZA "Meets monthly to hear requests for variances"; Plan Commission "meets monthly to process Zone Map Changes (rezoning) and Subdivisions"). The county Building Department "serves all properties within the City of South Bend", so building permits are county; who handles South Bend zoning administration was not confirmed.
- Not confirmed: South Bend Plan Commission/BZA membership and appointers. Search snippets (2019-2021 city documents) said 9-member Plan Commission with Mayor appointing 5 citizens and council/park board/public works board/city engineer one each, and 5-member BZA; not used, low confidence.

## 4. Animal control (filled)

- South Bend city limits: South Bend Animal Resource Center, 521 Eclipse Place, 311 or 574.233.0311; surrenders limited to residents of South Bend.
- Rest of county: Humane Society of St. Joseph County, Department of Animal Control: "contracted to perform animal control and sheltering services for St. Joseph County, Mishawaka City, and the towns of Osceola, Lakeville, New Carlisle, North Liberty, Notre Dame, St. Mary's, and Holy Cross. We do not service South Bend city limits." (574) 255-4726. WVPE 2026-03-30: commissioners approved the 2026 contract, $915,000.
- County also has an Animal Care and Control Commission (agendas on county site; membership not found). Mishawaka has an Animal Control Commission (2 council, 2 mayor appointees; 4 members, from the city's 2025 disclosure PDF).
- Gap: Walkerton and Roseland are not named in the Society's contract list.
