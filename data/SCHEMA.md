# Data schema: Indiana County Civic Guide

The site is generated from these files. **Indiana-wide data** lives in `data/indiana/` and is shared by every county edition. **County data** lives in `data/editions/st-joseph/`. A new county edition = new `data/editions/<county>/` folder with the same files.

## Universal rules
- CSV, UTF-8, comma-separated, header row exactly as specified, RFC-4180 quoting (quote any field containing a comma, quote or newline). Dates `YYYY-MM-DD` (use `YYYY` or `YYYY-MM` when that's all that's known, and say so in `notes`). Money in plain dollars with no `$` or commas (`393342.90`). Percentages as numbers 0–100 with up to 2 decimals.
- Every row carries `status` and `source_ids`.
- **`status`** (exactly one):
  - `official`: certified or official government record (county canvass marked Official, statute text, adopted budget from Gateway/DLGF)
  - `unofficial`: election-night or uncertified (any file whose header says Unofficial; 2026 general rows whose contest awaits state certification, a recount window, another county's votes or confirmation of the court). Put the "as of" date, or the reason the row is still unofficial, in notes.
  - `computed`: our arithmetic from other rows (percent from votes, sums, per-capita)
  - `from_scan`: transcribed from a scanned image (OCR or visual); notes say which
  - `secondary`: reliable secondary source (Wikipedia, Ballotpedia, reputable news) with no primary found
  - `single_source`: only one secondary source and not corroborated
- **`source_ids`**: semicolon-separated IDs from your area's sources file, e.g. `RES-012;RES-040`.
- **`confidence`** (where the file has the column): `high` / `medium` / `low`. Low-confidence facts are kept in data but flagged; the site won't show them in headings or charts.
- **Apply `planning/fact-check-report.md`.** Wherever it CORRECTED, CONTRADICTED or resolved a CONFLICT for something you're encoding, use the corrected value and cite the fact-check's source. Where it says REMOVE, don't encode the item. Log every fact-check finding you applied in your area's `_corrections_applied.md`.
- Never encode private individuals' addresses, phone numbers or e-mails.
- Neutral wording in every text field (no "flip", "sweep", "landslide", "seized", "won back", "wave", "safe", "target", "vulnerable"). Party labels: `D`, `R`, `L`, `I` (independent/no party), `G`, `NP` (nonpartisan), `W` (write-in), `O` (other; say which in notes).

## Sources (one file per area, merged later)
`sources_<area>.csv`: `id,url,title,publisher,accessed,kind,notes`. `kind` is `primary|news|secondary|statute|dataset|derived`. ID prefixes per area: RES (results), GOV (government/terms), MON (money), COM (community), PPL (people/images), IND (Indiana-wide), GAP (gap-fill).

## Indiana-wide (`data/indiana/`)
- `unit_types.csv`: `unit_type,label,plural,what_it_does,elected_bodies,can_levy_tax,statute,status,source_ids`
- `office_types.csv`: `office_type,label,unit_type,term_years,cycle,partisan,term_limit,vacancy_rule,statute,what_it_does,status,source_ids` (`cycle` = `even|odd|municipal|n/a`)
- `election_rules.csv`: `topic,rule_key,plain_language,detail,effective_from,effective_to,statute,status,confidence,source_ids` (calendar, primaries, straight-ticket by year, ID, absentee, petitions, caucus vacancies, school-board party labels, retention, citizen initiative, etc.)
- `statewide_offices.csv`: `office,what_it_does,term_years,elected_or_appointed,status,source_ids`
- `statewide_terms.csv`: `person,office,party,start,end,selected_by,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (governors as far back as sourced; current statewide officers; 2026 winners marked unofficial)
- `general_assembly_control.csv`: `year,chamber,d_seats,r_seats,other_seats,total_seats,supermajority_party,status,source_ids,notes`
- `state_local_rules.csv`: `topic,plain_language,detail,statute,effective,status,confidence,source_ids` (home rule, tax caps, levy controls, LIT, SEA 1-2025, HB 1210-2026, DLGF role, school funding basics)
- `indicators.csv`: `indicator_id,label,definition,universe,source_dataset,table_variable,moe_rule,display_rule`

## County edition (`data/editions/st-joseph/`)
- `edition.json`: county config (name, fips, seat, gateway code, edition color, data_as_of, variants: see `planning/ux-review.md` §5.3). Written by the main session.
- `units.csv`: `unit_id,unit_type,name,parent_unit_ids,places_covered,website,status,source_ids,notes` (county; cities; towns; 13 townships; school corporations; libraries; special districts)
- `offices.csv`: `office_id,unit_id,office_type,title,district,seats,elected,partisan,term_years,next_election,status,source_ids,notes`
- `terms.csv`: `term_id,office_id,person,party,start,end,selected_by,first_selected_by,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (`selected_by` = `election|caucus|appointment|unknown`; `end` blank = current; future terms starting 2027-01-01 from 2026 results are included: `official` only when the term rests on an official county-only result and cites RES-048 (the official county canvass of 11/13/2026), otherwise `unofficial`)
- `appointed_boards.csv`: `board_id,unit_id,name,what_it_decides,seats,appointing_authorities,levies_tax,status,source_ids,notes`
- `appointed_board_members.csv`: `member_id,board_id,person,role,appointed_by,term_start,term_end,holdover,as_of,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (one row per seat holder on an `appointed_boards.csv` board, added 2026-11-18). `member_id` = `<board_id>__<slug of the person>`, unique; a vacant seat is `<board_id>__vacant-<seat>` with `person` blank and notes saying "Vacant seat". `person`, `role` and `appointed_by` as printed. `term_start`/`term_end` ISO dates (a month or year when only that is printed); blank when the printed term is not a date, and then notes carry it as "Term: ..." ("Term: indefinite."). `holdover` = `yes` when the printed term has ended and a later official record shows the person still serving, else blank. `as_of` = date of the evidence (meeting or packet date, else the page's "last updated" date, else the read date). Never party, home address or contact details. Never show more filled seats than members listed (South Bend's Board of Public Safety has three member rows; the law allows 3 or 5 seats).
- `unit_contacts.csv`: `gov_id,body,phone,office_address,contact_url,website,meeting_rule,meeting_place,meeting_info_url,schedule_year,as_of,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (how to reach a government body's office and when it meets; added 2026-11-18). `gov_id` = a `units.csv` unit_id, or a money id resolved through `derived/unit_id_aliases.csv`; (`gov_id`, `body`) is unique. Office contacts only: `phone` is an office line as printed, `contact_url` one contact page or form, or `mailto:` for an official office address (never a personal mailbox; no cell may match `@(gmail|yahoo|sbcglobal|aol|hotmail)\.`), blank where the owner decided not to publish (Greene and Indian Village phones, Indian Village meeting place, 2026-11-18). `meeting_rule` = the stated rule with its qualifiers, or blank, never inferred from a list of dates. `schedule_year` = `YYYY` or `YYYY-YYYY` the rule was printed for, or blank. `as_of` = ISO date the page was read. `status` `official` (the body's own or a government page) or `secondary`. `notes`: reviewer-approved notes only.
- `elections.csv`: `election_id,date,type,scope,registered,ballots_cast,turnout_pct,straight_party_d,straight_party_r,straight_party_other,status,source_doc,source_ids,notes` (`type` = `primary|general|special|municipal_primary|municipal_general`; `scope` = countywide or which cities voted)
- `results.csv`: `election_id,contest_id,office_id,contest_label,district,party_primary,candidate,party,votes,pct,winner,vote_for,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (`party_primary` = party ballot for primaries, blank for generals; `pct` computed if not printed, and then the note says so; public questions use candidate=`YES`/`NO`)
- `ballot_questions.csv`: `contest_id,wording,wording_kind,status,source_ids,notes` (the wording of a public question, added 2026-11-18). `contest_id` = a `results.csv` contest_id, unique. `wording` = the question verbatim from the source, starting at the question itself (a record's "Public Question #1" heading is not part of it). `wording_kind` = `official_record` (state or county record of the question as voted) | `dlgf_approved` (wording a DLGF order approved; printed ballot not seen) | `sample_ballot` (the county sample ballot) | `reconstructed` (not used). `status` `official`. `source_ids` from `sources_results.csv` or `sources_elections.csv`. `notes`: caveats (multi-county question, printed ballot not seen).
- `overall_results.csv`: `contest_id,candidate,candidate_in_source,party,overall_votes,overall_pct,overall_winner,overall_total_votes,derived,electorate,vote_for,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (added 2026-11-18). The overall result (district-wide, statewide or whole-corporation) of a contest whose electorate extends beyond St. Joseph County, shown beside the county portion in `results.csv`. One row per contest and candidate or option that has a figure; contests with no figure are listed in `gaps_results.csv`. Districts and electorates wholly inside the county have no row (the county figure is the whole result). `contest_id` = a `results.csv` contest; `candidate` is spelled exactly as in `results.csv` when the candidate is there (the join key), otherwise as the state prints it (declared write-ins with no county row, party `W`); `candidate_in_source` = the state's spelling. `party` uses the labels above. `overall_votes` = the state's figure. `derived` = semicolon list of the columns that are our arithmetic, not printed by the source (`overall_pct`, `overall_winner`, `overall_total_votes`): show these with the site's computed mark ("share of votes for the candidates listed in the state's results; computed"). `overall_total_votes` = the sum of the lines the state lists (it prints no contest total and no aggregate write-in line); `overall_pct` = votes / that total (blank for school boards); `overall_winner` = most votes (`Y`/`N`; for a retention or question, the option with more votes). `electorate` = who voted (the state's jurisdiction text, or the district). `vote_for` as in `results.csv`. `status` `official` = official results as reported to the Secretary of State (Indiana Election Division historical results portal); never described as certified; no 2026 general row may be official. `overall_votes` is never below the county figure for the same candidate unless the notes say "county figure exceeds the overall" and why; the overall comes from a different record than the county canvass, so small differences are named in notes and the overall need not contain exactly the displayed county figure. `tools/check_results.py` enforces these.
- `budgets.csv`: `unit_id,year,basis,amount,adopted_or_actual,series_id,status,source_ids,notes` (`basis` = `general_fund|advertised_all_funds|all_funds_incl_utilities|gateway_reported_funds`, plus two table-only bases that are never charted: `dlgf_certified` = the total of the funds in the DLGF budget order, and `amended_all_funds` = a unit's current amended budget for all the funds it controls, from a later financial report). `adopted_or_actual` = `adopted|proposed|amended|series_break`; only `adopted` rows of the four main bases are charted, every other row goes to the "Other reported figures" table. Add `series_break` rows (amount blank) where the reporting basis changes, with an explanation in notes.
- `budget_breakdowns.csv`: `unit_id,year,kind,category,amount,basis,status,source_ids,notes` (`kind` = `spending|revenue`)
- `spending_breakdowns.csv`: `unit_id,year,view,category,amount,share,basis,status,source_ids,notes`. Generated by `tools/build_spending.py` from the Gateway AFR files (never hand-edit; re-run the script). Money in and money out per government, actual dollars, years 2024 and 2025 (schools: the state file year is the start of the school year, so 2025 = 2025-26). `unit_id` = the money-file ids in `budgets.csv`. `view`:
  - `income` (money the unit took in as its own, ≤6 categories + `Other`), `income_excluded` (money in that is not counted, by kind), `passthrough_in_detail` (funds inside the "collected for other governments" kind, top 6 + `Other`);
  - `by_purpose` (counted spending by the fund's purpose; schools by functional class), `by_type` (counted spending by object class; civil units only, the school file has no such split), `spending_excluded` (money out that is not counted, by kind), `passthrough_detail` (as above, money out).
  - Kinds used in `*_excluded`: collected for other governments and passed on; payroll withholding and clearing accounts; self-insurance and other internal service funds; pension, trust and custodial funds held for others; moved between the unit's own funds (transfers, loans, investments); reported by another unit as its own; borrowed (bond and loan proceeds, money in only).
  - `share` = percent of that view's total (2 decimals). Per unit and year, `income` + `income_excluded` = the receipts file total and `by_purpose` + `spending_excluded` = the disbursements file total, to the cent (the script fails otherwise); `by_type` sums to `by_purpose`.
  - `basis` = `afr_{year}_actual_all_funds_{sboa_chart|named_review}` (spending) or `afr_{year}_receipts_all_funds_{sboa_chart|named_review}` (income); `sboa_chart` = funds excluded by SBOA chart-of-accounts number ranges, `named_review` = no usable chart for that unit, exclusions reviewed fund by fund by name. A `_held` suffix (notes start `HELD`) marks a receipts year known to be misreported; the site shows the prior year instead.
  - `status` = `computed`. `source_ids`: MON-060/MON-061 disbursements 2025/2024, MON-063/MON-064 receipts 2025/2024, plus the chart used (MON-062 + MON-068 county, MON-065 schools, MON-066 cities and towns, MON-067 libraries).
- `tax_rates.csv`: `unit_id,taxing_district,year,rate_per_100,rate_kind,status,source_ids,notes`
- `assessed_values.csv`: `unit_id,year,net_assessed_value,basis,fund,status,source_ids,notes`. Generated by `tools/build_assessed_values.py` (never hand-edit; re-run the script; test in `tools/tests/`). Net assessed value, the property-tax base, in plain dollars. `year` = budget year, i.e. taxes payable that year, on assessments made the year before (the 2026 order is headed "2025 payable 2026"). `unit_id` = the money-file ids in `budgets.csv`. `basis`:
  - `dlgf_certified`: the "Certified AV" in the county's DLGF certified budget order for that year (2020-2026; MON-074, MON-073, MON-072, MON-071, MON-070, MON-027, MON-026). `status` = `official`.
  - `gateway_budget_estimate`: the "Net Assessed Valuation" the unit entered with its budget in Gateway (MON-001; 2012-2026; zero or blank entries are not written). An estimate made before values are certified, often rounded; in 2026 these ran 36% below to 3% above the certified values. Never chart it as the tax base or mix it with `dlgf_certified` on one axis; each row's note gives its difference from the certified value where both exist.
  - `fund`: the fund whose AV stands for the unit. AV can differ by fund within a unit (fire territories, fire-protection and debt funds, school referendum funds are levied on other areas): the General fund (0101) is used; schools use Operations (3300; before 2019 Gateway's school General fund 0101); special units their GENERAL fund, else their only AV. Notes list every other AV the order shows for the unit. Cross-county school corporations show the St. Joseph County part only.
- `debt.csv`: `unit_id,year,debt_type,amount,status,source_ids,notes` (`debt_type` text says what the amount is; rows whose `debt_type` starts "Total end-of-year principal" are the Gateway annual-report totals that the site charts; a unit's own reported total, such as South Bend's monthly report or budget page, has its own wording and date and is listed, not charted)
- `cf_state.csv`: `committee,candidate,office,district,party,year,receipts,expenditures,cash_end,top_funder_types,status,source_ids,notes` (state legislators from the state bulk data)
- `cf_local.csv`: produced later from `research/cf/` (Phase B)
- `indicator_values.csv`: `geo_type,geo_id,geo_name,indicator_id,value,moe,vintage,pulled_at,status,source_ids,notes` (`geo_type` = `county|place|town|county_subdivision|school_district|state|nation`; `place` = the two cities, `town` = incorporated towns) School indicators (defined in `data/indiana/indicators.csv`): `school_enrollment_total` (IDOE, `vintage` "IDOE enrollment, school year 2025-26"; IDOE's sheet "2026" = school year 2025-26), `school_spending_per_student` (DLGF, `vintage` "DLGF per-student expenditure, calendar year 2025"; calendar year, total expenditures / ADM) and `school_spending_per_student_median` (`geo_type` `state`, the median corporation). These rows are added by hand edits, not by `tools/build_indicator_values.py`, which would drop them if re-run.
- `employers.csv`: `name,sector,employees,as_of,selection_rule,status,source_ids,notes`
- `timeline.csv`: `date,category,title,description,status,confidence,source_ids` (`category` = `political|economic|government|legal|population`)
- `audits.csv` (optional): `unit_id,report_no,report_type,period_start,period_end,filed,auditor,findings,amount_charged,amount_questioned,referred_to,summary,status,source_ids,notes` — State Board of Accounts (or contracted CPA) reports per unit, shown in a "State audits" section on the unit's place page. `findings` = semicolon list of finding headings as printed in the report; `amount_charged` / `amount_questioned` = dollars as printed in the report's Summary of Charges / Questioned Costs (blank if none); `summary` only where the report's findings were read in full, attributed to the report. List every report on file for a unit the same way.
- `people.csv`: `person_id,name,offices_summary,parties,criteria,in_office_now,summary,documented_findings,image_id,status,confidence,source_ids,notes` (`criteria` = semicolon list of `TENURE|FIRST|HIGHER|FAMILY|TURNING_POINT`; `documented_findings` only for official findings such as court rulings, censures or settlements, attributed)
- `images.csv`: `image_id,subject,file_page_url,direct_url,license,credit,local_file,notes`
- `research_on_voting.csv`: `study_id,authors,title,outlet,date,geography,period,method,finding_summary,limits,url,status`
- `gaps.csv`: `area,item,why_missing,how_to_fill`

## Conventions added by gap-fill round 2 (2026-11-18)
- **Hand-maintained since 2026-11-08; generators retired.** `results.csv`, `elections.csv`, `sources_results.csv`, `units.csv`, `offices.csv`, `terms.csv`, `appointed_boards.csv`, `sources_gov.csv`, `budgets.csv`, `debt.csv` and `sources_money.csv` are edited by scripted, logged merges (the dated sections of `_corrections_applied_*.md`; the 2026-11-18 merge is `planning/gapfill/merge/merge.py`). `tools/res_main.py`, `tools/build_gov.py` and `tools/build_money.py` are stale against these files and must not be re-run: a rebuild would drop the hand edits. The three new files above have no generator.
- **results.csv, 2026 general (`2026g`)**: every row carries the official county canvass figure of 11/13/2026 (RES-048). A row is `official` only if it cites RES-048 (county-only contests and House Districts 5, 6 and 8). An `unofficial` 2026g row's notes name the canvass and say why the contest is still unofficial: it awaits state certification (statewide, appellate, public-question, multi-county district contests), the recount window, or the school corporation also votes in another county (John Glenn, LaVille/Union-North, New Prairie). `tools/check_results.py` enforces both.
- **budgets.csv / debt.csv**: `school-new-prairie` and `school-union-north` rows are whole-corporation figures, and every such row's notes contain "Whole corporation (principal county: ...)" (LaPorte, Gateway county 46 for New Prairie; Marshall, Gateway county 50 for Union-North budgets; Union-North debt: Marshall, its annual financial report filed under St. Joseph County, Gateway county 71). `debt.csv` holds AFR Debt years 2022-2025: per year the class rows by unit, then each unit's total and due-in-one-year rows; one row per (unit, year, debt_type); each unit-year "Total end-of-year principal outstanding (all debt)" equals the sum of its "End-of-year principal outstanding: ..." class rows. The county and Redevelopment Authority totals for 2024 and 2025 carry a note never to add the two (the same $50,000,000 bonds are in both). `tools/check_money.py` enforces these.
- **Federal and statewide units**: `units.csv` has `us-house` and `us-senate` (`unit_type` `federal_chamber`, `data/indiana/unit_types.csv`) and `in-state` (statewide executive offices and appellate courts) so their offices have a unit_id. `data/indiana/office_types.csv` adds `us_representative`, `us_senator`, `supreme_court_justice`, `court_of_appeals_judge`, `tax_court_judge` and `superintendent_public_instruction`. They are not local governments.
