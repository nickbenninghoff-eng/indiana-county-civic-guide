# Budgets and Finances - St. Joseph County, Indiana (research notes)

Prepared 2026-11-07. Collect-and-report only; no conclusions. Dollar figures are nominal US dollars.
Search budget: 22 of 25 WebSearch calls used. Most numbers were pulled directly from state data (Indiana Gateway bulk download) and from the cities' own budget PDFs, then cross-checked against news.

## 0. Read this first: data definitions and conflicts

Three different "budget" measures appear in news and official data. They are NOT interchangeable.

1. **Gateway "Budget Data" (Form 4B budget estimate, adopted column)**: fund-level adopted budget for funds advertised to the state (DLGF). Summed across funds here ("advertised funds"). Source: https://gateway.ifionline.org/public/download.aspx (Finance and Budget Files > Budget Data). The page is a form, but a form POST returns a pipe-delimited file; all years 2012-2026 were pulled and filtered to county code 71. "Year" = budget year.
2. **News / official "total city budget"**: South Bend's about $417M (2024) and about $424M (2025) are all-funds figures that include water/sewer utilities, grants and enterprise funds (per ABC57).
3. **General fund only**: e.g., county "just over $90 million" in WNDU/WVPE matches Gateway county General fund 2026 adopted estimate of $90,704,400.

**Conflict, St. Joseph County 2026:** WNDU (2025-09-09/10) says the balanced 2026 budget is "just over $90 million" while the auditor is quoted as "a balanced general fund budget at $97 million, which is 39% of the total budget". Gateway shows General fund adopted estimate $90.70M (published/advertised $91.95M) and the sum of all advertised county funds $248.2M (97/0.39 is about $249M, so the quoted "total" appears to be all funds, about $248M). WNDU's "$90 million" therefore corresponds to the General fund, not the whole county budget. (WNDU's text "increase of more than $1,400, 1% up" is internally inconsistent; probably $1.4 million.)

**Conflict, South Bend 2024-2026 in Gateway:** Gateway shows about $427M for South Bend Civil City in 2023 (includes sewer, water, trash and others) but only about $166-171M for 2024-2026 because only 10 property-tax-supported funds are listed from 2024 on. The Gateway series has a **series break in 2024**. For 2024-2025 use the news all-funds figures (Section 2). No all-funds 2026 or 2027 total was found.

**Mishawaka:** Gateway sums match the city's own budget books (2022 $59.34M; 2024 $70.27M vs $70.25M in the book; 2025 $76.99M; 2026 $77.92M). These are "Civil City" budgets and EXCLUDE the electric, water and wastewater utilities (Section 3).
