# Re-check of the 2010–2019 county canvasses (2026-11-09)

Nick, 2026-11-09: "Can you try doing these?" — re-check the 2010–2019 county canvasses that the fact-check pass could
not re-derive (sjcindiana.gov blocks scripts).

## Method
1. **Download.** The 17 county result PDFs (DocumentCenter 60004–60020, sources RES-001…RES-018) were fetched inside a
   Claude-in-Chrome tab on sjcindiana.gov (same-origin `fetch` + blob download; Cloudflare passes a real browser).
   Copies: `sources/results/county-browser-2026-11-09/`. All are scans (no text layer); about 650 pages.
2. **Percent cross-check.** results.csv stores the printed percentage separately from the votes, so each row's
   votes ÷ contest total was compared with its printed percentage: 6 rows in 4 contests disagreed. A sum-to-100 check
   found 2 contests with missing candidates.
3. **Independent OCR.** Every page was OCR'd with Tesseract (4x render). A row counted as confirmed when its exact vote
   count and the candidate's surname were on the same OCR line: **1,294 of 1,398 rows**.
4. **By eye.** The other 104 rows were cropped from the page images (labelled with our value) and read; any
   disagreement was re-read at higher resolution before changing anything.

## Limits
- The percent cross-check tolerates ±0.05 points, so in big contests it cannot catch a misread of a few votes; the OCR
  pass and the eye check are what confirm those.
- results.csv is also produced by `tools/res_main.py` (not run; off-limits). These corrections are hand edits; a
  rebuild from res_main would need the same fixes.
