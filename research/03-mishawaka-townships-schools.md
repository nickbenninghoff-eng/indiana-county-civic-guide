## A. City of Mishawaka

### A1. Structure
- Form: mayor-council. Mayor (executive), 9-member Common Council, elected City Clerk. [H] https://mishawaka.in.gov/government/elected-appointed-officials/
- Council: 9 members = 6 district seats + 3 at-large seats. Voters choose up to 3 at-large candidates. [H] https://mishawaka.in.gov/government/elected-appointed-officials/common-council/ ; [M] WNDU 2023 results.
- Council meets 1st and 3rd Mondays; has a Council Attorney (Mike Trippel); controls city property, finances, appropriations. [H] same page.
- Election cycle: odd years (2023, next 2027). Term length (4 years) is not stated on the pages fetched; the 2023 results and "fourth full term" language are consistent with 4-year terms [L - confirm via Indiana Code IC 3-10-6 / city site].
- Boards listed on city site: Parks and Recreation, Board of Public Works and Safety (and Utility Board), Board of Zoning Appeals, Historic Preservation Commission, Plan Commission, Redevelopment Commission, Traffic Commission. The page does NOT say who appoints; it links to a "2025 Appointing Authority Disclosures" document (not read). [H] https://mishawaka.in.gov/government/elected-appointed-officials/ . One fact: the Common Council has an appointee on the Plan Commission (Matt Carroll for 2026). [M] search result, https://www.mishawaka.in.gov/government/elected-appointed-officials/boards-commissions/plan-commission/
- Vacancy rules: **not found** (not searched beyond the above).
- Mishawaka school board links: 2 of 5 School City seats are appointed, one by Common Council and one by the mayor (see C).
- Mishawaka seats on county boards: Board of Health has a seat appointed by the Mishawaka mayor (currently vacant; see D).

Union Township office is in Lakeville (in.gov/townships/union71). (Wikipedia notes Greene Township joining John Glenn was discussed in 2022.)

### B2. What townships do (Indiana)
Each township has one trustee plus a three-member township board [M-H: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_township ]. Services documented on township pages: Harris Township lists parks, cemeteries, assistance (poor relief), fire and rescue; board "mainly approves budgets," trustee "delivers services" [H: https://harristwp.com/about ]. Centre Township: fire protection and poor relief [H: https://centretownshipin.gov/ ]. Even-year, partisan, 4-year-term election rules: trustee terms listed as ending 12/31/2026 (BallotReady), consistent with 2026 election; **statutory text (IC 36-6) and assessor-history not fetched**. Township assessors were historically elected; history not researched.

### C2. Partisan law
Senate Enrolled Act 287 (2025), signed by Gov. Braun May 6, 2025, effective July 1, 2025: candidates may list a party, "independent," or remain nonpartisan; no primary required; first applied Nov 2026 (~800 seats statewide). Two-party rule: candidate's last two primaries must match party sought. July 2026 Chalkbeat: about half of 1,034 candidates statewide listed no party; confusion in the statute threatened two IPS candidates' ballot spots. Sources: https://www.chalkbeat.org/indiana/2026/07/27/confusion-on-partisan-schoolboard-threatens-ips-ballot-spots/ ; https://news.ballotpedia.org/2025/06/10/indiana-to-become-10th-state-to-hold-partisan-school-board-elections/ ; https://www.wfyi.org/news/articles/senate-narrowly-sends-bill-creating-partisan-school-boards-races-to-governors-desk . Bluewater/South Bend Tribune piece lists local 2026 candidates with party labels (not retrieved; 403).
