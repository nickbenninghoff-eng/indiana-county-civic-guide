# St. Joseph County, Indiana: official election results, 2020-2026

Compiled for a nonpartisan civic-education guide from the County Election Board page https://www.sjcindiana.gov/2392/Election-Results. Summary/canvass totals only (no precinct detail). Federal races (president, US Senate, US House) are omitted from the tables; straight-party totals are included. Percent = candidate votes divided by the sum of candidate votes in that race (computed; for multi-seat races it is each candidate's share of all votes cast in the race). "Winner" is shown only for single-seat races; multi-seat races are marked "multi-seat". In primaries, Y means the leading vote-getter in that party's primary.
