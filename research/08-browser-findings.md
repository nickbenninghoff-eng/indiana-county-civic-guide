# Browser research findings (main session, Claude in Chrome) — 2026-11-07

## St. Joseph County Candidate Filings page
URL: https://www.sjcindiana.gov/2330/Candidate-Filings
- County posts SOME local CFA-4 (receipts/expenditures) and CFA-11 (large-contribution notice) filings as scanned images (not text PDFs). Coverage posted online: 2023 Annual → 2026 Pre-General. Only a subset of committees appear per period (e.g. 2023 Annual tab: IRP, Michael Compton, Mueller only).
- Readable via screenshot + zoom; no text layer.
