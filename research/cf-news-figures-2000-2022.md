# News-reported local campaign finance figures, St. Joseph County IN, elections 2000-2022 (collected 2026-11-09)

Summary: 16 WebSearch calls used of 40 (stopped early; returns had dropped to near zero). 30 table rows (about 20 distinct figures) found (table A), all for South Bend mayor (2007, 2011, 2015, 2019) and 2022 Penn-Harris-Madison school board. Nothing found for Mishawaka mayor, any council, any county office, SBCSC, Mishawaka/John Glenn/New Prairie/Union-North boards. Sources checked: Wikipedia (2007/2011/2015/2019 mayoral pages, with citations), Howey Politics Indiana / Howey Political Report PDFs (read in full text via Wayback copies of in.gov/library files), South Bend Tribune via Yahoo syndication, Center for Public Integrity, The Federalist, Washington Free Beacon, WNDU (Wayback copy), ABC57, Ballotpedia, Wayback copies of the county website. Not reachable: southbendtribune.com (blocked, and the Wayback copy of Tribune pages was refused by the tool), WVPE/WSBT/WNDU live pages (no finance hits in search), ndsmcobserver.com (HTTP 403 to fetch), web.archive.org CDX index (offline).

Method note: the Howey PDFs are the most productive single source. They are weekly digests that reproduce Tribune/WNDU finance numbers verbatim. The in.gov/library URLs are dead (404) but Wayback has the PDFs, e.g. `https://web.archive.org/web/2/https://www.in.gov/library/files/HPI_20190425.pdf`. Only issues I could retrieve were searched; a Howey issue not retrieved may hold more.

## B. Archives of older local reports

1. County website, before and after (Wayback, retrieved via archive.org availability API and page copies, 2026-11-09). The county site was `sjcindiana.com` (CivicPlus) until about 2020-2024, now `sjcindiana.gov`.
   - Page "Candidate Information" (Clerk > Voting & Elections): `http://sjcindiana.com/887/Candidate-Information`. Captures: https://web.archive.org/web/20191219235819/http://www.sjcindiana.com:80/887/Candidate-Information (Dec. 19, 2019) and https://web.archive.org/web/20200812062820/http://sjcindiana.com/887/Candidate-Information (Aug. 12, 2020).
   - What it lists under "Finance Documents": only the blank forms "Committee Statement of Organization" (DocumentCenter/View/8179) and "Report of Receipts and Expenditures of a Political Committee" (View/8180), plus Declaration of Candidacy (13306), Statement of Economic Interest (13307), "Official 2018 Primary results" (14947), and pages "2019 General Election Candidates" (/1192) and "Upcoming Dates for 2019 Election". NO filed candidate CFA-4 reports are listed in either capture. I did not open the DocumentCenter files.
   - Related pages found: Voter Registration (`/211/Voter-Registration`, capture 20191218105459), Clerk (`/180/Clerk`, capture 20191218104711), Voting & Elections (`/232/Voting-Elections`, capture 20191219105801). The Voter Registration page links "Candidate Information" and a "Voter Calendar" but no finance reports.
   - Earliest `sjcindiana.gov` capture found: May 2, 2024; no `.gov` capture of the candidate-information page found for 2023. The older domain `co.st-joseph.in.us` has a capture from Oct. 12, 2003 (not examined). No capture of an older `/1073/Candidate-Filings` page found (availability API returned nothing for that path under .com or .gov).
   - Not done: a full Wayback CDX listing of sjcindiana.com paths (the CDX service was offline), so unseen captures may exist.
2. Record retention: The county destroyed Buttigieg's 2011 reports. The Federalist (Sept. 23, 2019) says the clerk's office confirmed it and cited a four-year retention schedule; it said Luecke's 2008-2010 reports and 2011 council-candidate reports still existed at that time. Center for Public Integrity (Aug. 8, 2019) repeats that the county destroyed the 2011 forms under state law. URLs: https://thefederalist.com/2019/09/23/exclusive-who-shredded-buttigiegs-campaign-records-and-why/ and https://publicintegrity.org/politics/pete-buttigieg-mayor-indiana-money-lobbyists/ . Implication: the county may not hold filed reports older than roughly four years even if asked (not confirmed for current practice).
3. Ballotpedia (SBCSC 2014 page, search-result text only; page fetch returned empty): "The St. Joseph County Election Board does not publish and freely disclose school board candidate campaign finance reports." https://ballotpedia.org/South_Bend_Community_School_Corporation_elections_(2014)
4. Howey Politics Indiana / Howey Political Report archive: weekly PDFs 2007-2019 at `https://www.in.gov/library/files/HPI{yymmdd}.pdf` (2011, 2015) and `HPI_{yyyymmdd}.pdf` (2019), and `HPR14nn.pdf.pdf` (2007). Live URLs 404 now; Wayback copies retrievable. This is the best public archive of Tribune-reported finance numbers. Retrieved and searched (South Bend finance content only where noted in table): HPR1403/05/06/07/08/10/11/13/14 (2007); HPI 110303, 110414, 110421, 110428, 110505, 110512, 110602, 111013, 111027, 111103, 111110 (2011); HPI 150115 to 151112 selected issues (2015); HPI_20190411, _0425, _0502, _1031 (2019).
5. Indiana Election Division bulk data: confirmed in the earlier note that local committees are not in it. Not re-checked here.
