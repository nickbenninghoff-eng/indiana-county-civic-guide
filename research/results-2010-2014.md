Only the countywide SUMMARY pages were extracted; precinct pages were skipped. Federal races (President, US Senate, US House) are omitted; straight-party totals are included where printed.

Conventions: votes with a trailing "?" are OCR-uncertain. Winner = highest vote-getter(s) for the seats shown (nominee in primaries); for retention and public questions the leading side (Yes or No) is marked Y. Multi-seat boards: the top-N vote-getters are marked Y, with N taken from the "vote for not more than" line. In primaries each group is one party's ballot. Percent is as printed on the report unless noted. "Statewide (county tally)" means a statewide race with only St. Joseph County's votes shown. OCR commonly read comma as period (e.g. 3.756 for 3,756); these were normalized and cross-checked against the printed percentages.
